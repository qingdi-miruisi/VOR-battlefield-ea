\caption{Convergence behaviour (median IGD over 3 seeds, log scale, as a
function of function evaluations). \textbf{Left}, LSMOP6 (collapse
instance): EDD reaches its final IGD of $1.629$ already at $G=25$ and
does not improve further, whereas MOGWO --- its only competitor in final
quality --- needs the full 200 generations to reach $1.633$, and every
other algorithm remains two to three orders of magnitude away at the full
budget. \textbf{Right}, LSMOP2 (converged instance): EDD's IGD
\emph{degrades} monotonically over the budget
($0.3321 \to 0.4194 \to 0.4249$) while MOEAD and RVEA improve slightly
and stay near $0.09$. This is the trajectory-level signature of the
ablation result (b): the EED path does not merely fail to help on LSMOP2,
it actively pulls the population off the front as generations accumulate.
No other algorithm in the study exhibits a degrading trajectory on this
instance.}
